ABOUT THE APP

Meet the founder

Study Compass is a study-planning and focus app designed to help you build a steady routine. It brings together a Pomodoro timer with short and long breaks, task lists, reminders, study-time analytics, and session history.

Plan your next steps, stay focused in manageable sessions, remember what needs doing, and see the progress you make over time. Your study data stays in your browser.
